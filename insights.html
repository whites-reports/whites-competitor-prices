Whites growth insights

Five things hiding in Whites' own order data and in the daily competitor price checks. Each comes with the money involved and what to do about it.

Orders: 1 Nov 2025 – 26 Oct 2026 (Whites backend export, all online orders). Competitor prices: daily checks 28 Oct – 6 Nov 2026. SAR, VAT included.
1 · Payday calendar

Average sales by day of the month (100 = a normal day)

Average sales by day of the week

What Acquisit advises
  • Launch the biggest campaigns and new offers on the 26th–27th, so they are live when salaries land.
  • Use the quiet 16th–24th window for targeted flash deals and reminders instead of broad discounts.
  • Weight ad budget towards Friday and Saturday.
2 · Baskets and bundles

Brands most often bought together (orders)

What the few Dyson and Clara buyers who add something pick (orders)

What Acquisit advises
  • Offer a ready-made "complete the look" add-on at checkout for every Dyson and Clara order: a heat protectant, a hair mask (K18, Kérastase) or a makeup set.
  • Turn the strongest pairs into bundles, for example Priorin + Cystine as a hair-vitamin pack, and Beauty of Joseon + La Roche-Posay as a sun-and-repair set.
3 · Life after hair tools

October runs to 26 Oct.

Brands moving fastest (sales per 30 days, Aug–Oct vs May–Jul)

What Acquisit advises
  • Put ad budget behind the brands already growing on their own: L'Oréal Professionnel, ISDIN, Kérastase, Medicube and QV.
  • Investigate the fall in La Roche-Posay, Whites' biggest beauty brand. It is also where competitors are cheaper (see the price dashboard).

What Acquisit advises
  • Pick the 15–20 products customers compare most and hold a stable "everyday price" within 5% of Nahdi, instead of switching offers on and off.
  • Avoid ending many offers on the same day, as on 1 Nov, when Whites went from cheapest on 13 products to 5 overnight.
